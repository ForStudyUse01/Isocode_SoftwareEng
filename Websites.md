# Websites

Every website scraped, searched, used, mimicked or cloned in this project.

| Website | How it was used | Notes |
|---------|-----------------|-------|
| fonts.googleapis.com / Google Fonts | Used. Inter and Noto Sans Devanagari are fetched at build time by `next/font` and then self-hosted | Open licence. No request to Google from the browser |
| Figma AI image generation (via the Figma connector) | Used. Generated the four car images in `public/cars` | Invented unbranded cars, no plates or badges. Used the account's Figma AI credits |
| api.anthropic.com | Used, optional. Writes lead summaries and reply drafts when `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is set | Receives first name, needs and budget. Never phone or email |

Nothing was scraped, cloned or mimicked. Car imagery is AI-generated for this project and shows invented cars. Prices, taxes, cities, showrooms, staff and leads are invented sample data. Tax rates and fees are illustrative and must be replaced with the dealer's figures. The one rule taken from general knowledge is TCS at 1% above ₹10 lakh, which should be confirmed with the dealer's accountant.
